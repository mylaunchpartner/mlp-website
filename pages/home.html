FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to sign up for all four services?

No. Many clients start with one department, often a website or CRM build, and add campaigns and creative once the foundation is in place. The big advantage is that every piece you add plugs straight into what's already running.

Is the work done in-house or outsourced?

Our media buyers, video editors, designers, developers and CRM specialists all work as one My Launch Partner team, under one project lead and on one shared roadmap.

Which CRMs and platforms do you work with?

We build and manage CRMs in GoHighLevel, HubSpot and Keap (Infusionsoft), including custom setups, automations, integrations and migrations between platforms. If you're on another system, we'll work around the tools you already use. For ads we run Meta, Google and YouTube, and for email and SMS we work inside your CRM or dedicated email platform.

How fast can we get started?

After a strategy call we send a scoped proposal. Once it's approved, kickoff and the audit usually start within days, so you're not waiting months the way you would on a new hire.

How do you report on results?

Because we build and run your CRM, reporting ties back to real leads and revenue, not just clicks. You'll get regular updates and a dashboard that shows the full journey from ad to closed deal.
