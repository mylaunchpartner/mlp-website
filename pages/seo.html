FAQ

SEO questions, answered.

How long does SEO take to work?

It depends on how competitive your searches are and how much your site needs fixed. Brand and low-competition local searches can move in weeks. Louisville Lead Inspections reached page 1 two weeks after launch. Competitive national searches take months of steady content and links.

What does performance-based SEO mean?

You only pay performance fees when the searches we agree on reach page 1 of Google. If a keyword isn't on page 1, you don't pay for it. Read the full breakdown on our performance-based SEO page.

What's in the $1 SEO audit?

Where you rank today for the searches that matter to you, a site health and speed check, how you compare with your top competitors, your Google Maps presence and a short list of the fixes that will move the needle first. You get a written report and a walkthrough call.

Do you guarantee a #1 ranking?

No one can honestly guarantee a specific position, because Google decides. What we guarantee is the risk: if your keywords don't reach page 1, you don't pay performance fees for them.

Do you work with local businesses or national ones?

Both. Local businesses need service-area pages, a strong Google Business Profile and reviews. Online and national businesses need topic coverage, content and links. The audit tells us which plan fits you.

Can you do SEO on GoHighLevel, WordPress or Shopify?

Yes. We work on the platform you already have, including GoHighLevel, WordPress and Shopify. If your platform is holding you back, we'll tell you in the audit.
