FAQ

Performance-based SEO, answered.

What is performance-based SEO?

It's SEO where you pay for results instead of time. We agree on the searches you want to rank for, and you only pay performance fees for the ones that reach page 1 of Google.

What counts as page 1?

The top 10 organic Google results for the search, checked on our weekly ranking check. Paid ads and the Google Maps pack don't count.

Who chooses the keywords?

We choose them together on your audit walkthrough call, before any work starts. They're searches that bring you real customers, not vanity terms nobody types.

What happens if a keyword drops off page 1?

Performance fees follow page 1. If a search drops off page 1, you don't pay performance fees for it while it's off.

How is this different from a normal SEO retainer?

A retainer charges the same every month whether you rank or not, so you carry the risk. With performance-based SEO, we only earn performance fees when your searches are on page 1, so we're paid for the same thing you want.

How do I get started?

Start with the $1 SEO audit at mylaunchpartner.ai/audit. It shows where you rank today and what to fix, and on the walkthrough call we'll tell you whether performance-based SEO is a fit.
