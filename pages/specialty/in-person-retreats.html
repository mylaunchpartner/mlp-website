FAQ

In-Person Retreats questions, answered.

How far ahead should we start marketing a retreat?

The earlier the better. We plan the launch calendar backward from your retreat date, starting with a waitlist, and adjust it to your price point and number of spots.

Can guests pay in installments?

Yes. We set up checkout with deposits and payment plans, plus automatic reminders before each payment, so you're not chasing balances.

Do you film the retreat?

Our video editors work from footage you or your venue provide. We'll give you a shot list ahead of time so the recap content is ready for your next launch.

Can you handle several retreats a year?

Yes. Once the funnels and CRM are built, each new retreat reuses the same system with new dates, pricing and creative.

Which CRM do you build in?

We build and manage CRMs in GoHighLevel, HubSpot and Keap (Infusionsoft). If you're already on one, we'll work inside it. If not, we'll recommend the best fit for your team and sales process on the strategy call.
