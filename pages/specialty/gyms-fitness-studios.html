The Challenge

A full schedule starts with a steady flow of trials.

Most gyms don't have a lead problem. They have a follow-up and retention problem.

Trials that never convert

People claim the intro offer, take a class or two, then disappear without a follow-up.

Ads that bring the wrong leads

Broad targeting and weak offers fill your inbox with people who never show up.

Front desk can't keep up

Calls, texts and DMs pile up during class times and new leads go cold.

Quiet cancellations

Members drift away and nobody notices until the payment stops.

FAQ

Gyms & Fitness Studios questions, answered.

Can you connect to our booking or membership software?

We connect your CRM to your scheduling and membership tools wherever they support it, through native integrations, Zapier or Make, or APIs. We'll review your setup on the strategy call.

What intro offer should we run?

It depends on your price point, class type and capacity. We'll look at your numbers on the strategy call and recommend an offer to test.

Do you work with multi-location gyms?

Yes. We build location-specific ads, pages and pipelines so each location gets its own leads and reporting.

Which CRM do you build in?

We build and manage CRMs in GoHighLevel, HubSpot and Keap (Infusionsoft). If you're already on one, we'll work inside it. If not, we'll recommend the best fit for your team and sales process on the strategy call.
