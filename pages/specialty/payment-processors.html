FAQ

Payment Processors questions, answered.

Can you run Google Ads for financial services?

Yes, with the right setup. Google requires some financial services advertisers to complete verification first, and we'll walk you through what applies to your business before anything launches.

Do you help recruit sales agents or ISOs?

Yes. We build a separate recruiting funnel and pipeline for agents, with application, onboarding and training automations.

Can you connect our CRM to our boarding platform?

Wherever your platform supports it, yes, through native integrations, Zapier or Make, or APIs. We'll review your stack and tell you what's possible.

Which CRM do you build in?

We build and manage CRMs in GoHighLevel, HubSpot and Keap (Infusionsoft). If you're already on one, we'll work inside it. If not, we'll recommend the best fit for your team and sales process on the strategy call.
