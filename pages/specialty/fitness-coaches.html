FAQ

Fitness Coaches questions, answered.

How does video editing work?

You film and upload your raw clips to a shared folder. Our editors handle cuts, captions, hooks, music and formatting for each platform, then send them back for your approval.

Do I need a big following to run ads?

No. Ads put your content and offers in front of new people, so you don't need a large audience to start. We'll recommend a starting budget and offer on the strategy call.

Can you work with my coaching app?

We connect your CRM to the tools you already use wherever they support it, through native integrations, Zapier or Make. We'll review your setup on the strategy call.

Which CRM do you build in?

We build and manage CRMs in GoHighLevel, HubSpot and Keap (Infusionsoft). If you're already on one, we'll work inside it. If not, we'll recommend the best fit for your team and sales process on the strategy call.
