Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Who we are

My Launch Partner LLC, an Arizona limited liability company ("My Launch Partner," "we," "us"), is a marketing, creative, web and CRM operations agency based in Sedona, Arizona. This policy explains what information we collect through mylaunchpartner.ai (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions can go to hi@mylaunchpartner.ai.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you book a call, fill out a form or email us, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, website and anything you tell us about your business.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the Site and the tools that run it automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring page and the date and time of your visit. This is collected through cookies, pixels and similar technologies.

How we use your information

  • To schedule and hold calls you book and to respond to your questions.
  • To send you information you asked for, proposals, appointment reminders and follow-ups.
  • To provide, manage and bill for services if you become a client.
  • To send marketing emails or text messages, only where you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time.
  • To understand how the Site is used, measure our advertising and improve our services.
  • To keep the Site secure and comply with the law.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and consent to receive text messages, we may text you about appointments, your inquiry, our services and account updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except service providers that deliver messages on our behalf.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as our CRM, scheduling, email and SMS delivery, website hosting, payment processing and analytics providers. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • With advertising and analytics platforms (for example Google and Meta) through cookies and pixels, to measure and improve our ads.
  • When required by law, to protect our rights or safety, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of our business.
  • With your consent.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the Site (such as the booking calendar) may not work properly without them. You can opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in any email, and out of texts by replying STOP. You can also learn about limiting ad personalization through your Google and Meta account settings.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as needed for the purposes above, including to provide services, keep business records and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No website or system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, email hi@mylaunchpartner.ai. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and may need to verify your identity first.

Children

The Site is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

The Site may link to websites we don't control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
