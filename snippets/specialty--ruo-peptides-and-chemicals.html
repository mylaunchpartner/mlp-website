FAQ

RUO Peptides & Chemicals questions, answered.

Can you run Meta or Google ads for RUO products?

Both platforms heavily restrict peptide and research chemical advertising, and their policies change often. We'll review what's possible for your catalog, but we build your growth plan around channels you own (email, SMS, search content and your CRM) so it doesn't depend on ad approvals.

Will you write our product descriptions?

Yes. We write spec-focused, research-use-only copy covering purity, testing, storage and documentation. We don't write dosing instructions or health claims.

Can you help us get through a payment processor review?

We can't approve you, but we can make sure your site looks the way high-risk underwriters expect: clear terms, refund and shipping policies, RUO disclaimers, real contact information and a professional checkout.

Which CRM do you build in?

We build and manage CRMs in GoHighLevel, HubSpot and Keap (Infusionsoft). If you're already on one, we'll work inside it. If not, we'll recommend the best fit for your team and sales process on the strategy call.

Can research peptide companies do SEO?

Yes, and it's one of the few channels you can grow on, since most ad platforms restrict research-use-only products. Every page we write stays inside research-use-only language with no health or dosing claims.

Will SEO content put my merchant account at risk?

Not when it's written correctly. We keep product and blog content compliant with research-use-only rules, the same standards processors check for when they review your site.
